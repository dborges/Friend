PostPilot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. What We Collect

PostPilot collects the following data when you connect your TikTok account:

  • TikTok Open ID: A unique identifier for your TikTok account, used to associate your account with your PostPilot session.
  • Access Token & Refresh Token: OAuth tokens used to publish content to your TikTok account on your behalf. Stored securely and never shared.
  • Basic Profile Info: Display name and username, used to identify your account within PostPilot.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • user.info.basic: Used to identify your TikTok account and display your profile information within PostPilot.
  • video.publish: Used to publish scheduled video content to your TikTok account at times you specify.
  • video.upload: Used to upload video files to TikTok's servers prior to publishing.

3. Data Storage

OAuth tokens are stored securely on PostPilot's servers. We do not store your TikTok video content beyond what is necessary for scheduled posting. Content is deleted from our servers once it has been successfully published.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or TikTok account data with third parties, except as required by law.

5. Third-Party Services

PostPilot integrates with the TikTok API. TikTok's own Privacy Policy governs data processed by TikTok's platform.

6. Your Rights

You may disconnect PostPilot from your TikTok account at any time. Upon disconnection, all stored tokens for your account will be deleted within 24 hours.
